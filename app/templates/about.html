About Us

Astro Pitch Database helps casual players, five-a-side and club teams, schools and community groups find an astro pitch that suits their group.

What you can do

  • See which pitches are open at the time you want to play
  • Find a pitch size that fits a smaller or larger group
  • Register, log in and review the pitches you have played on
